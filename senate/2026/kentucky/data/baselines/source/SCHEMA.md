# Kentucky partisanship source and calculation schema

Observed 2026-10-20. This package has complete county certificate controls and qualified native observations. **Canonical full-state native order, top 15 and minimum 80 are null; dispatch keys are empty.** No current precinct identity, geometry, ballot roster, eligible named-write-in allocation, live collector/cadence, remaining pool or calibrated probability is established.

`pins.json` freezes individual original-body roles once. Each record identifies the copied complete receipt, receipt hash, original read-only body path relative to the Kentucky state directory, decoded/stored SHA256 and byte lengths, role, URL, hop and observed status. A gzip body reconstructs the complete decoded HTTP response entity with `gzip.decompress`; no original is truncated or allocated. All 120 county recap responses were individually verified HTTP 200 PDF bodies. Legacy receipts retain final entity headers/status/URL/UTC; unobserved intermediate hops and wire/TLS bytes remain unknown. New `own-*` receipts preserve attempted hop/error evidence. Their initial sandbox DNS failures and subsequent direct 403s remain separate. Acquisition does not depend on a mutable aggregate.

The certificate original bytes are reused read-only from `data/news/` through exact hashes: 2024 `69f32f69255f65c9e9ed7b05a1a60a7c17f6bcc8444548a1a34e6f936b42c6b1`; 2022 `46b46cbad74d2c6a7cc03a30a85bac02e2e8d0c60ed02cd4b9e9f66ccedd64e5`; 2020 `66bb0eedd13f43b09b2e42f9b6119bf292e6361b0657fc4e2ab56907c3511773`. Multiple archive observations returning identical entities are not additional votes or release chronologies. PDF metadata dates do not establish release/amendment dates. Current SBE 2024 navigation labels the certificate amended December 9, 2024.

`extracted/<hash>.layout.txt.gz` preserves **all pages and offices**, formfeeds, headers and footers from each complete PDF. `pdfinfo` page controls close 133 PDFs/17,073 pages; the 120 recaps account for 16,246 pages. Full originals remain the authority. `bbox.html.gz` preserves each complete attempted bounding-box extraction output, including partial failed outputs. A nonzero `bbox_exit` in `document-inventory.json` marks that output unusable as a complete coordinate representation; its stderr is retained. Poppler's rotated-text bounding-box crash does not affect the complete layout extraction. Layout locators are 1-based page/line and 0-based character offsets, not polygon coordinates. All glyphs/spelling remain in originals; clearly marked manual display transcriptions are separate.

`certificate-fields.csv` contains all 3,480 county candidate positions and 29 state-footer positions: `source_sha256,page,line,column,char_start,raw_token,raw_line,votes,candidate_key,scope,county_literal,year`. A positional candidate key is `<original-hash>:column:<1-based-position>`; duplicate display names would remain separate. Source county labels are literal. `candidates.json` maps each source column to its manually read candidate/party/running mate; the complete raw headers are in `certificate-headers.json`. President and vice president share **one vote column**. The 17/4/8 columns retain Independent, Kentucky Party, Libertarian and every named write-in identity; no opposition pool is renamed Democratic. `certificate-controls.json` separately records all 120 county column sums and printed footer values. These tables do not report ballots, registration or voting methods: those certificate fields are null, never zero.

`native-observations.jsonl.gz` preserves selected presidential and administrative lines, raw text, all token spans, county, source hash, page/line and literal page-unit header. Observation keys are `<hash>:p<page>:l<line>`; they identify source positions, not normalized precincts. `native-page-contexts.jsonl.gz` retains every 16,246 recap page, including unrelated-office header appearances; it does not create a numerical second derivative of unrelated offices. Choice reports put TOTAL last; Electionware summary reports put TOTAL first. Multiline method headers remain in full originals/layout, page context and raw observations. Source-native units may be precincts, splits, central counting groups or administrative labels; no name-range assumption or crossyear native join is made.

`native-positional-fields.jsonl.gz` adds integer count positions to selected rows. A parent `Write-In Totals`, its named children and `Not Assigned` overlap. They are preserved individually and **never all summed**. Cast-vote, contest-total, undervote and overvote controls remain distinct; a blank/unreported field is not a printed zero. A raw-context parser is intentionally more inclusive than a semantic presidential table. In Laurel/Madison, its complete source rows do not establish an accepted additive candidate total, so those contexts are excluded from quantitative subset arithmetic and retained in `native-unresolved-pair-contexts.json`. No residual cells are reconstructed.

`native-known-pair-units.json` accepts only unique literal-unit A/B observations with one printed cast-vote control, or an explicitly labelled sum of six regular source choices plus the generic write-in parent. Each retains its exact source-row keys, pair participation, total basis and unknown 2026 identity/full-named-field closure. These 3,143 source contexts include 23 zero-pair contexts with undefined preference, and do not establish a complete universe. Scott's10 page report is cumulative, with null native label. Magoffin's source TOTAL is Mail In-only. `source-reconciliation.csv` compares every 120 certificate parent with the own accepted known recap subset; missing aggregate values are blank, not zero. Disagreements are not repaired.

`elliott-manual.json` is independent visual transcription of 7 presidential/native blocks after inspecting all 16 complete pages. It retains all 6 regular candidates, generic write-in parent and unassigned child, cast-vote control, registration/ballots/blank and the source clock. The generic 3 marks are not the certificate's named-write-in 0. Original text is image-only. `ocr-<hash>/` stores complete TXT/TSV/HOCR derivatives for Elliott and the 5 Estill/Lyon 2020 scanned pages; OCR is not numeric truth. Contact sheets and selected complete renders are in `proof/`. Estill's sideways second page and distinct local-write-in third page, overlapping Mail-In/Walk-In/Total/Scan template, blanks and uncertain handwriting are retained; no complete accepted historical native numerical allocation is claimed.

`turnout-registration-positioned-controls.jsonl.gz` preserves all nonempty source lines/tokens in the pinned 2020/2022/2024 county/native turnout and 2024 registration PDFs, including overlapping native/district/admin/footer rows. `county-admin-turnout.csv` extracts all 120 county controls for each year: Total/D/R/Other registration and Number voting, original percentages and source positions. Registration party is **not candidate preference**. `county-registration.csv` extracts all 120 county party/precinct-count/registered controls; unrelated demographic columns remain only in full originals/raw lines. The registration PDF says September 2024 although its URL says November General 2024. Administrative Number voting is not certified ballots or contest votes; active/inactive/eligible-person definitions remain source-qualified. No native join to recaps is assumed.

`data/county-baselines.csv` has 360 county-year rows, source-local county spelling/positions, independently associated Census county FIPS, named A(D)/B(R), all candidate vector, pair/all totals, A pair share, pair participation, pair margin and full-field share. Blank ballot/registration cells mean not supplied by the certificate. Census FIPS association is county-only, including explicit case variations such as Larue/LaRue; it is not a native identity crosswalk.

`county-order.csv` retains all 120 descending 2024 certificate contest-volume rows. Equal volumes sort by Census FIPS; no ties affect this observed order. Integer crossing uses `5*cumulative >= 4*state_total`, and verifies the preceding row fails. The county sensitivity in `priority.json` is not canonical native dispatch. `native-subset-scope.json` lists every included positive-pair key and all 23 zero-pair/undefined contexts, with unresolved contexts linked separately.

`state-scenarios.json`, `county-targets.csv` and `scenarios/*` use all 120 parents. Historical low/base/high volumes and county mixes are 2022/2024/2020 **all-candidate certificate counts** respectively; each preference anchor independently supplies its county pair participation and A share. Thus pair volume is volume-year contest total times preference-year participation. No source value is rescaled or crossyear native unit joined. `native-known-subset-*` uses own source-local 2024 volumes/preferences plus independent Elliott manual blocks, separate from state arithmetic. It includes 3,127 positive-pair contexts and retains unknown full-native units outside its scope. The native sensitivity's unallocated-other label is an arithmetic container, not an eligible candidate or Democratic coalition.

Targets include pair weight/contribution, clipped share/margin, net gain, one-share-point net-vote value, full-candidate volume/participation and selected-only required A share. County selected-only rows fix every other county at the unshifted stated anchor/mix; native rows fix every other known-subset context and explicitly cannot solve unknown statewide units. Raw required shares below 0 or above 1 remain `secured`/`impossible`. Uniform unbounded algebra and feasible clipped roots are separate. Net votes, pair-margin points and A-share points are different quantities.

`peer/` contains complete selected captured stable turnout witness bodies, receipts, schemas and exact scope/hash records. Own original-first witnesses precede this capture. `peer/comparison.json` describes only that bounded frozen comparison; `differences.jsonl.gz` retains all differences. There is no future-final equality, peer-value repair or peer-model adoption. The two original-first scopes did not supply an accepted final canonical native dispatch contract.

`fixtures/` retains actual CLI inputs, strict-JSON stdout and exact exit/stderr records. Three deliberately malformed nonfinite JSON input fixtures are expected to fail; no calculator output contains NaN/Infinity. `proof/calculator-validation.json` checks real CLI behavior and independently enumerates exact rational piecewise clipping segments. `history/` preserves prior failed checker/parser/script/output/error bodies before narrow corrections. Current `asset-manifest.json` includes every owned file except itself, including histories, witnesses, validation and finalseal. The seal does not hash the current manifest, so there is no reverse digest cycle. Final audit is read-only.
